CRT afterglow effects (to do)

Recreations of real effects on cathode-ray tubes, where the phosphor keeps glowing after the beam has passed: the origin of the name AfterGlow. Each card says how it could be used.

1. Radar phosphor (P7)

Hit by the sweep: a blue-white flash, then a long yellow afterglow.

Use: a pulse that flashes blue-white and cools into the app's colour.

2. Oscilloscope trail

The moving dot leaves a fading line where it has been.

Use: an optional setting that leaves faint trails behind moving things.

3. Switching off

The picture collapses to a line, then a dot that glows and dies.

Use: pausing or quitting collapses the wallpaper the same way.

4. Burn-in ghost

An old image stays faintly visible behind the new one.

Use: a jelly marked seen leaves a faint ghost; the clock keeps last minute faintly.

5. Scanlines and bloom

Bright parts bleed light; dark lines between the rows.

Use: an optional CRT overlay for the wallpapers.
