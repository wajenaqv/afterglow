signals from your own scripts

          
While AfterGlow runs it listens on this computer only. Requests from web pages are refused.

          
curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:47811/signal -d '{"source":"build","kind":"new"}' 

          

            
sourcea name: whatsapp, gmail, build, … (known apps pulse in their own colour)

            
kindnew, unread (with count), clear or flash

            
colouroptional #rrggbb for the pulse

            
afterglowoptional seconds the pulse takes to fade (default 4)
