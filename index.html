# connectors

      
Let your apps nudge the wallpaper. AfterGlow only reads which app and how many, never what a message says.

      


      
rules

      
What each app's messages do. * is any app without its own rule; apps with no rule pulse in their own colour and count as waiting.

      

        
appnew messagecolourcounts as waiting

        
      

      
 


      
# signals

      
Try the reactions without any apps connected. A message pulses one jelly (or sends a cat) in that app's colour; things waiting bring in more jellies.

      

        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      

      
from your own scripts: curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:47811/signal -d '{"source":"build","kind":"new"}'
